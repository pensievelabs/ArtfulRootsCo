Why This Tool Exists

I wanted my child to understand math, not fear the buzzer.

When looking for math apps for my young child, every top-rated option was built like a stressful speed arcade. Red countdown clocks, loud buzzer sound effects for wrong answers, and endless confetti animations designed to provoke adrenaline rather than deep understanding.

In Montessori classrooms, children don't use timers. They handle wooden beads, place tokens in wooden tens frames, and build spatial intuition with their hands. I built Clean Math as a software engineer and mindful parent to bring that exact tangible calmness to digital screens.
