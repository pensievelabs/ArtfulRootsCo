Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy 🛡️

At Artful Roots Co., we believe that learning should be a safe, calm, and private experience. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users, including children. There are no accounts to create, no email addresses required to use our apps, and no tracking of your child’s progress outside of your own device.

Data Collection and Usage 📥

Our applications are 100% offline-first. Any progress data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your phone or tablet. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or any software that tracks user behavior.

Any device permissions—such as access to your camera or location—are used strictly with your permission for on-device processing only (for example, scanning a QR code or adjusting your location on a map). Sensor data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any of our backend servers.

Outgoing Links & Third-Party Services 🤝

Our applications contain no in-app purchases, advertisements, or links to third-party websites or social media platforms. Any outgoing links contained within our apps point exclusively to this website (artfulroots.co).

Specifically, our apps may include direct links for parents to download free printable worksheets or to access parent support.

If you contact us for support via our website or email, we will only use your email address to respond to your inquiry and will never sell, rent, or share your contact information.
