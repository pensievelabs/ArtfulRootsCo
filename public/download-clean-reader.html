The Hidden Problem

The "Educational" Casino

We are sold a lie that children need flashing lights to learn. But most reading apps aren't teaching your child—they are harvesting their attention.

Confetti & Games

They focus on gamification, streak counters, and digital rewards, leaving actual phonics and deep comprehension behind.

Animated Mascots

They cause sensory over-stimulation and hijack a developing nervous system instead of building intrinsic focus.

The Subscription Trap

You pay $10/month forever for a "set and forget" service, masking the true lifetime cost of a tool you never actually own.

The "Always Online" Leash

They require constant internet connections, rendering them completely useless on airplanes or long car trips without Wi-Fi.

The Data Harvest

They track and monetize your child's screen interactions, build profiles, and serve third-party incentives to maximize addiction.
