01 · Personal Vault

Create your home bank.

Set up your child's personalized home bank in seconds. Establish initial balances and teach them how real money is securely held and organized in one clean, dedicated place.

02 · Effort & Reward

Track chores and earnings.

Connect household contributions directly to earnings. Assign age-appropriate chores and let kids see how diligence builds their savings—without gamified noise or artificial points.

03 · Financial Transparency

Clear, honest ledgers.

Every dollar earned or spent is recorded in a simple, readable transaction ledger. Kids learn to track where their money goes and experience the clarity of a balanced record.

04 · Value Allocation

Spend, Save, and Give jars.

Cultivate purposeful financial habits. Seedling splits earnings into three distinct jars: Spend for immediate desires, Save for meaningful goals, and Give to foster generosity early in life.

05 · Thoughtful Decisions

Real-world spending choices.

Before handing over a debit card, guide your child through intentional spending choices. Seedling makes every purchase a deliberate, reflective action rather than a mindless tap.
