The Phonics Progression

From isolated sounds to independent reading.

Five purposeful stages designed to cultivate quiet self-efficacy and a lasting love for physical books.

01

Letter Sounds — Auditory Clarity

A clean Montessori A–Z soundboard. Blue for vowels, terracotta for consonants. When children tap, they hear crisp, natural phoneme articulation without loud mascot shouting or distracting sound effects.

02

Sound Blending — Tactile Mastery

A smooth, physical slider that lets children drag individual letter sounds together. They hear the transition happen in real time, making the core abstract mechanics of reading completely tangible.

03

Sight Words — Honest Confidence

100 high-frequency words with self-directed reflection. Children swipe based on their own confidence: "I know it" or "Practice again." No stressful timers, no lost lives, no shame.

04

Sentences — Reading in Context

50 decodable sentences structured around phonetic rules: Short-A patterns, consonant digraphs, and blends. Formatted cleanly like pages of a high-quality primer.

05

Decodable Stories — Pride of Independent Reading

30+ beautifully illustrated, fully decodable stories. Children read every word themselves using the rules they've mastered. The reward is the satisfaction of the story itself—not digital badges.
