The Home Routine

A daily ritual of calm learning.

We built this system to preserve the peaceful atmosphere of your home. Here is how mindful parents synchronize the printable worksheets with the Clean Reader app.

1

Audio First: Load the Phonemes

Open the Clean Reader app. Tap the letter sounds for today's worksheet. Your child hears the crisp, natural articulation before they touch pencil to paper. Audio loads the sound in memory.

2

Put the Screen Away: Tactile Pencil Focus

Set the tablet or phone face down. The physical worksheet is now the center of focus. Your child traces and writes with zero screen glare, digital pings, or notification distractions.

3

The Self-Check Retrieval

When stuck on a word like "ship," don't give the answer. Let your child return to the app for 10 seconds to tap the sound, self-correct through active retrieval, and return to paper.
